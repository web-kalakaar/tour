Featured package

3-Day Beawar Heritage & Circuit Tour

Our most-booked package — a three-day route through Beawar's Walled City, the Aravali hill roads, and the Ajmer-Pushkar circuit. Below is exactly what happens each day, followed by full pricing across three comfort levels.

Day by day

The route we take you on

1
Day 1 — Morning to Evening

Arrival & Beawar Walled City Walk

Pickup and check-in, followed by a guided walk through the Parkota — entering via Ajmeri Gate, through Agarsen Market, to Mahadev Ji Ki Chhatri and Chang Gate. Evening free at leisure or a visit to Subhash Udyan.

2
Day 1 — Evening

Colonial Beawar & Local Sweets

Visit the Shoolbred Memorial Church, then a stop at Halwai Gali to try til patti, Beawar's signature sesame-jaggery sweet, before dinner and overnight stay.

3
Day 2 — Full Day

Ajmer & Pushkar Excursion

Early departure to Ajmer and Pushkar, roughly 70 km away. A full day covering the region's temples and lakeside sights, with lunch included, before returning to Beawar for the night.

4
Day 3 — Morning

Dev Dham Devmali & Aravali Drive

A scenic drive into the Aravali hills toward Masuda, visiting the sacred site of Dev Dham Devmali, before a final breakfast stop back in Beawar.

5
Day 3 — Afternoon

Handicraft Shopping & Departure

A final stop at the Walled City's handicraft stores for Rajasthani puppets and decorative pieces, followed by drop-off at your hotel, bus stand, or railway station.
