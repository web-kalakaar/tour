Frequently Asked Questions

Booking, pricing, safety, and what to expect — answered before you have to ask.

Send us an enquiry through the Contact page or WhatsApp with your preferred dates, group size, and package tier. Our team confirms availability and shares a payment link along with a written itinerary before you pay anything.

We accept UPI, bank transfer, and major debit/credit cards through a secure payment link. A confirmation advance is required to lock your dates, with the balance due before the tour begins.

At least one week ahead for standard packages. During the Tejaji Fair, Dussehra, and other peak-season dates, we recommend booking 3 to 4 weeks in advance as hotel availability tightens quickly.

Transport, accommodation, meals as specified per tier, and a guide are included in every package. Monument entry fees, personal expenses, and anything outside the listed itinerary are extra — see the Pricing section for a full tier-by-tier breakdown.

Yes. Hotel rates rise during the Tejaji Fair and Dussehra period, which can affect final pricing. We always confirm exact pricing in writing before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Children under 5 travel free (no separate seat or bed). Children aged 5 to 11 are charged at approximately 50% of the adult rate. There's no separate senior discount, but we're happy to adjust pacing for elderly travellers at no extra cost.

Cancellations made 7 or more days before the tour date receive a full refund minus a small processing fee. Cancellations within 3 to 6 days receive a 50% refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are non-refundable. Full details are on our Cancellation Policy page.

Yes, and we'd prefer it. One free reschedule is allowed with at least 48 hours' notice, subject to availability on the new date. Reach out on WhatsApp and we'll sort out new dates directly.

If we cancel a tour due to weather, safety concerns, or circumstances outside anyone's control, you'll receive a full refund or the option to reschedule at no additional cost — whichever you prefer.

Comfortable walking shoes are essential for the Walled City lanes. In summer (April to June), carry light cotton clothing, sunscreen, and a water bottle. In winter (October to March), a light jacket for early mornings and evenings is enough — days stay mild and sunny.

Yes. Beawar is a quiet, family-oriented town, and all our tours include a vetted local guide and driver. We're happy to share driver and guide details in advance, and our team stays reachable throughout your trip via WhatsApp.

The nearest major railway junction is Ajmer, about 70 km away, with regular onward buses and taxis to Beawar. The nearest airports are Kishangarh and Jaipur. If you let us know your arrival point, we can also arrange pickup as part of your package.

October through March offers the most comfortable weather for walking tours. If you want to see Beawar's cultural side at its liveliest, plan around the Tejaji Fair or Dussehra, when the Walled City hosts public performances and celebrations.

Yes, this is one of our most common requests. Share your dates, interests, and group size through the Contact page and we'll build a route around them, priced using the same transparent per-tier structure as our standard packages.

Our standard packages are designed for groups of 2 to 12. For larger groups, schools, or corporate outings, contact us directly — we regularly arrange multi-vehicle tours for 20 or more travellers.

Groups of 10 or more typically qualify for a per-person discount, since vehicle and guide costs are shared more efficiently. Reach out with your group size for an exact quote.
