The destination

Beawar — Rajasthan's Walled City

Founded in 1836 by Colonel George Dixon on the edge of the Aravali hills, Beawar grew from a trading outpost into one of Rajasthan's most distinctive walled cities. Its old town, the Parkota, is still entered through four historic gates — Ajmeri, Mewari, Chang, and Surajpole — each opening onto narrow lanes, markets, and havelis that carry the town's trading-era character.

Beyond the walls, the surrounding Aravali landscape and proximity to Ajmer and Pushkar make Beawar a natural base for exploring central Rajasthan's temples, forts, and desert scenery.
